# Id-free ledger search at scale

50 translated query documents per condition (committed NLLB run) searched against ledgers of the 50 registered documents plus K distractor documents drawn from the corpus generator (seed 20261023). Matching: contract-record index (equivalent to tag verification up to 2^-32). A query document is attributed to every registered document matched by >= 50% of its sentences. Wilson 95% CI.
